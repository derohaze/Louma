Receiving LMA is simple because it saves time. It is also sensitive because one mistyped address can send funds to the wrong place, create confusion, duplicate a payment, or leave a balance that no longer matches your records.

Louma treats receiving as a controlled flow, not a casual copy-and-paste action. Your balance and receiving address are too important to change without clarity, review, and accountability.

## What safer receiving should provide

A reliable receiving experience should give you confidence before, during, and after the transfer:

- Clear ownership by account.
- Validation of the receiving address.
- Reviewable feedback before funds are accepted.
- Visibility into progress and final outcome.
- Clear explanation of transfers that need correction.
- A dependable record of what arrived.

These guardrails help you move faster without lowering the quality of your records.

## Address quality affects the whole wallet

Address quality is not only a convenience concern. It affects transfer accuracy, expectations, settlement confidence, and the quality of your history.

| Address issue | Practical impact |
| --- | --- |
| Duplicate addresses | Creates confusion and inconsistent records. |
| Incorrect address data | Can send funds to the wrong place and reduce trust. |
| Missing labels | Makes filtering and searching less reliable. |
| Weak naming | Makes search and review harder. |
| Unclear results | Forces manual checking after every transfer. |

Louma is designed to reduce these risks by treating receiving as part of the same environment as transfers, mining, and history.

## Why formal review matters

The best receiving flow is not the one that accepts the most transfers. It is the one that helps you trust your balance after the work is complete.

A formal flow gives you a clear view of what is accepted, what needs correction, and what should be reviewed before it affects your balance. That clarity turns receiving from a risky action into a manageable process.

## The Louma approach

Louma's receiving direction is built around control, visibility, and accountability. You should be able to receive LMA at scale while still understanding the impact of each transfer.

For a growing wallet, that balance matters. Speed is valuable, but trusted records are what keep everything else steady.
