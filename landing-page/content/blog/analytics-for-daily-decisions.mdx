Wallet activity should help you decide what needs attention today. Reports that arrive late, depend on manual reconciliation, or stay disconnected from daily use often become background noise instead of a reliable tool.

Louma approaches reporting as part of the daily rhythm of using a wallet. The goal is to give you one dependable view of your balance, transfers, mining, and follow-up priorities.

## What useful reporting should answer

Strong reporting begins with practical questions:

- Which transfers require immediate attention?
- Which balances are moving unexpectedly?
- Which recipients account for the most activity?
- Which settlements are still pending?
- Which mining cycles are underperforming?
- Which settings need a second look?
- What changed since the previous day?

When these questions are visible in the same app that handles your balance, transfers, addresses, and history, reporting becomes directly connected to action.

## From numbers to management clarity

Louma reports are intended to support decisions, not decorate a screen. A metric is valuable when it helps you understand a situation, take responsibility, and act with confidence.

| Reporting area | Practical value |
| --- | --- |
| Transfers | Shows volume, status movement, delays, and points that need attention. |
| Balance | Highlights movement, unusual changes, and holdings that need review. |
| Recipients | Gives context for frequent contacts, history, and account activity. |
| Settlements | Makes pending confirmations visible before they become a problem. |
| Mining | Connects cycle performance to the rate you actually earn. |

This kind of reporting gives you a clearer view of your wallet without forcing you to collect evidence from several separate tools.

## Why context matters

Charts without context can lead to the wrong decisions. A rise in transfer volume may be positive, but it may also expose a settlement backlog. A strong mining cycle may increase earnings while creating more records to review. A delayed confirmation may look isolated until it is connected to a specific recipient, network, or time of day.

Louma is built to keep activity data close to context. That context helps you understand not only what happened, but where attention should go next.

## The Louma outcome

The most important reporting outcome is trust. You should trust that the numbers reflect your current balance, that exceptions are visible, and that daily decisions are based on the same source of truth.

For Louma, reporting is not a separate destination. It is a layer over the same wallet activity that happens every day.
