Every growing wallet needs a clear answer to one question: how does each account holder know their data belongs only to their own account?

For Louma, account separation is not a visual label. It is a core product requirement. Each account holder needs confidence that their balance, transfers, recipients, mining, history, and connected services remain organised inside the correct account.

## Account ownership is part of the product

Account data belongs to one account:

- Transfers and settlement activity.
- Recipient records and context.
- Balance and receiving address information.
- Mining cycles and rewards.
- History and confirmation exceptions.
- Connected service activity.
- Reports, summaries, and management views.

When ownership is clear, you can work faster without wondering whether you are viewing the right account or recipient context.

## Why isolation matters

Account isolation is often discussed as a technical concern, but the value is practical. One account holder should not see another account's transfer history. You should not review another account's report by mistake. A transfer should not affect the wrong balance.

Clear separation protects your data from accidental exposure and gives you confidence to expand your use of the wallet.

| Growth moment | Why isolation matters |
| --- | --- |
| Adding a new device | Each session needs clean ownership. |
| Adding connections | Provider activity should remain tied to the correct account. |
| Reviewing reports | Decisions need reliable, separated data. |
| Handling transfers | Recipient context must stay within the right account. |
| Using custom addresses | Each address should belong to one account. |

## Better structure for larger use

As your wallet grows, one account may include multiple sessions, repeated routines, and connected services. Without strong separation, daily work becomes risky and confusing.

Louma is designed to make account ownership visible and consistent. That structure supports clearer roles, cleaner reporting, safer connected activity, and more reliable use.

## The product benefit

Good isolation gives you confidence to grow. You can add devices, connections, reports, and routines without turning each step into a boundary concern.

For Louma, account isolation is part of what makes the wallet suitable as a daily surface. It protects trust while keeping the work organised.
